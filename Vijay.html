VIJAY

Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar, known mononymously as Vijay, is a prominent Indian actor, dancer, playback singer, and philanthropist, primarily working in Tamil cinema. Early Life and Background Birth: Vijay was born on June 22, 1974, in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. He hails from a film-oriented family; his father, S. A. Chandrasekhar, is a film director, and his mother, Shobha Chandrasekhar, is a playback singer. 2 Childhood: He began his career as a child actor at the age of 10 in the film Vetri (1984) and made his lead debut in Naalaiya Theerpu (1992). 2 3 Sources Career Highlights Filmography: Over his three-decade career, Vijay has acted in over 68 films, becoming one of the highest-paid actors in Tamil cinema. Some of his notable films include Ghilli (2004), Thuppakki (2012), Mersal (2017), Master (2021), and Leo (2023). 2 Awards: He has received numerous accolades, including eight Vijay Awards and three Tamil Nadu State Film Awards. He has also been featured multiple times in the Forbes India Celebrity 100 list. 2 3 Sources Personal Life Family: Vijay was deeply affected by the death of his sister, Vidhya, at a young age. He married Sangeetha Sornalingam, a fan, in 1999. 1 Philanthropy: He is known for his philanthropic efforts, including his fan club, Vijay Makkal Iyakkam, which engages in various social welfare activities. 2 3 Sources Political Aspirations In 2024, Vijay announced his retirement from films to enter politics, launching his political party, Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam. Wikipedia Popularity and Influence Vijay is often referred to as "Thalapathy" (Commander) by his fans and has a significant following not only in India but also in countries like China, Japan, and the UK. His films are screened in over 80 countries.
